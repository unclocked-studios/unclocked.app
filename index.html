Warehouse mapping service

Converting your AutoCAD layout into a coordinate table.

Unclocked 3D Heatmap uses a unique location name and numeric X and Y coordinates for each bin, with optional Z coordinates for height. If that information is still in your drawing, we can help prepare the CSV and work through the units, location labels, and rack levels with you.

01

Describe your drawing

Tell us the drawing format, approximate number of locations, and what you would like mapped. Estimates are enough to start the conversation.

02

Agree on the mapping details

We discuss how to share the drawing and confirm location names, measurement units, and the height of each level before mapping begins.

03

Receive the coordinate table

The agreed output is a CSV of location names and X, Y, Z coordinates that you can connect to your warehouse data in Power BI.
