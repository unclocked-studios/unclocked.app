twenty5 Support

Thanks for using twenty5. This page provides troubleshooting steps, FAQs, and support contact information.

Contact Support

Email us at support@unclocked.app and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

App Troubleshooting

  • Ensure you’re using the latest version of twenty5 from the App Store.
  • Try restarting the app or your device.
  • If issues persist, email us with your device info, iOS version, and a brief description of the problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does twenty5 sync with iCloud?

Yes. If iCloud is enabled on your device, your data can be securely synced and restored if you switch devices.

Does twenty5 access my Apple Health data?

Yes. twenty5 can read and write data to Apple Health — but only with your explicit permission, and only for body weight. This information is used solely within the app to enhance your tracking experience. Unclocked Studios does not collect, store, or share this health data, so you can rest assured it will never be sold to third parties.
