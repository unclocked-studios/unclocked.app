twenty5 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how twenty5, an iOS app from Unclocked Studios LLC ("we", "our", or "us"), handles your data. Other products from Unclocked Studios have their own privacy policies.

Information We Collect

Unclocked Studios LLC does not collect, receive, or store personal information through twenty5. The app does not require an account, and we do not collect your email address, device identifiers, workout data, or analytics.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your workout data is stored on your iPhone and, if you use the Apple Watch companion, on your Apple Watch. If iCloud is enabled, twenty5 can also sync and restore that data through your personal iCloud account. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple account and is not accessible to us.

Apple Health

twenty5 can read and write body weight in Apple Health, only if you grant permission. This information is used within the app to support your tracking. We do not receive, store, or share it, and you can change this permission at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

Your Choices

You can delete your data at any time by deleting the app or using in-app reset features (if available). To remove data stored in iCloud, use your Apple account's iCloud storage settings.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions, please contact us at support@unclocked.app.
