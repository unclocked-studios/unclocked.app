Feature highlights

Explore large warehouse layouts without leaving Power BI.

Layout builder Experimental

No coordinates needed. Draw racks, bays, and levels over your floor plan, and the visual creates every Location ID and places your data on it.

Playback and compare Professional

Add a date or period to replay the heatmap week by week, or color each location by how much it changed.

Cutaway

Slice the warehouse at any level to see inside the racks, or show a single level on its own.

Congestion floor Professional

Shade the aisles in front of busy pick faces to see where pickers crowd, not just which shelves are busy.

Flexible heat mapping

Color locations with any numeric measure, as a gradient or bands, with thresholds that can follow your slicers.

Search, select, and save views

Find a Location ID, drag to select an area and see its totals, and save up to 100 named camera views.

How it works

Bring your warehouse into Power BI.

1. Place every location

Provide a unique Location ID with X and Y coordinates for each bin, or draw the racks in the layout builder and let the visual create the locations.

2. Add the measure to analyze

Map inventory, picks, utilization, aging, capacity, quality holds, or another operational metric.

3. Explore in report context

Use slicers, filters, search, the cutaway, playback, and saved views to investigate patterns.
