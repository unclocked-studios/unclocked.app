A few helpful details

Before you get started.

What if my drawing doesn’t include every level?

Tell us what information is available. Level heights and missing location information can be discussed before the mapping scope is agreed.

Will this page convert my drawing automatically?

No. This page helps you prepare a request for the studio. We review the drawing and requirements with you before mapping begins.

Can I request dimensions or other fields?

Include any additional requirements in your description. The core output is location names and X, Y, Z coordinates; other fields are discussed during scoping.

How do I submit the request?

Prepare the draft, then review and send it in your email app. If you use webmail, copy the request and email it to support@unclocked.app with the subject “Warehouse mapping request.” This website does not send the email or store your request.
