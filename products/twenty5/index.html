Workout tracking without subscription clutter.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does twenty5 cost?

twenty5 is free to download, with no subscription and no account to create.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone with iOS 18.0 or later. The Apple Watch companion needs watchOS 11.0 or later.

Does twenty5 sync between devices?

Yes. If iCloud is enabled, your workouts sync through your personal iCloud account and can be restored when you switch devices. Your Apple Watch and iPhone stay in sync while you train.

Does twenty5 use Apple Health?

Only for body weight, and only if you allow it. Unclocked Studios does not collect, store, or share that data.
