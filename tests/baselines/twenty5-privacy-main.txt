        <h2>Introduction</h2>
        <p>This Privacy Policy explains how Unclocked Studios LLC ("we", "our", or "us") handles user data across all of
            our applications, including <strong>twenty5</strong> and any future products we release.</p>

        <h2>General Information We Collect</h2>
        <ul>
            <li>Email address (used for account identification and support)</li>
            <li>A unique, anonymous user ID tied to your Apple ID (used solely to prevent trial abuse)</li>
        </ul>

        <h2>How We Use Data</h2>
        <p>We use collected information strictly for providing app functionality and improving our services. We do not
            sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.</p>

        <h2>App-Specific Information</h2>
        <h3>twenty5</h3>
        <p>
            Workout tracking data is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, securely in your personal iCloud
            account.
        </p>

        <h3>Apple Health</h3>
        <p>
            With your permission, twenty5 can read and write your body weight in Apple Health. twenty5 does not request
            access to any other Health data.
        </p>
        <ul>
            <li>Body weight from Apple Health is used only within twenty5 to support your workout tracking and progress.</li>
            <li>Unclocked Studios does not receive, store on its servers, sell, or share your Apple Health data, and it
                is never used for advertising or marketing.</li>
            <li>You can review or turn off twenty5’s access at any time in the Health app or in your iPhone’s Settings.
                Turning off access does not delete data that is already stored in Apple Health.</li>
        </ul>

        <h3>Future Apps</h3>
        <p>
            Future apps will have their specific practices described here. Any additional data collected will always be
            disclosed and handled transparently.
        </p>

        <h2>Your Rights</h2>
        <p>You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app or using in-app reset features (if available).
