Frame64 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Introduction

Frame64 is a Chrome extension created by Unclocked Studios LLC that converts images to Base64, optimizes them, and exports code. This Privacy Policy explains how Frame64 handles data.

Image Processing

Frame64 processes your images and PDFs locally in your browser. Images, PDFs, and the Base64 or code they produce are not uploaded to Unclocked Studios LLC or to a conversion server, and we do not receive them.

Local Storage

Frame64 stores settings, presets, saved recipes, and workspace state locally in your browser using Chrome extension storage. This data stays on your device and is used only to provide Frame64's features.

Clipboard and Web Page Images

Frame64 reads the clipboard only when you choose to paste, and writes to it when you choose to copy. If you send an image from a web page to Frame64 using the right-click menu, your browser requests that image directly from its original address, without cookies or a referrer. Frame64 does not send the image or its address to us.

Trials and Payments

The free version does not require an email address. To start a trial or purchase a plan, Frame64 uses ExtensionPay and Stripe. Your email address is used to track the trial and your plan, and is kept private and never sold. Payment processing is handled by those services, and Frame64 does not receive or store credit card numbers or payment details. Please review the privacy practices of ExtensionPay and Stripe for how they handle that information.

Analytics and Tracking

Frame64 does not include analytics or advertising trackers and does not collect usage activity.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@unclocked.app.
